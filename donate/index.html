Support Dutchman

Hi! I’m Ray (raykholo on GitHub), and I built Dutchman.

I used proprietary CAD software for 15 years and had a lot of designs stuck in those formats. I was also moving from Windows 10 to Linux, with no interest in Windows 11, which meant I couldn’t run that software even if I wanted to. When SolidCraft appeared, I set out to bring all those designs across without losing what makes them editable: the feature history, the constraints, the design intent. That’s Dutchman.

Along the way I’ve become one of the biggest contributors to SolidCraft (issues triaged and code merged) and to cadmpeg, the open-source CAD format reader Dutchman uses under the hood.

All of this runs on AI plans and build and test hardware. The more I can run, the faster Dutchman and SolidCraft improve. If Dutchman has saved you time, a donation helps keep it going.

To be clear: donations go to me personally to support my work. They are not donations to SolidCraft, cadmpeg or Tangency Labs.

See how to donate on our Discord

It’s entirely voluntary. If you can’t or would rather not, no problem: reporting problems, sending test files and telling people about Dutchman help just as much.

Also by me: Cobblr, no-file-left-behind, and the RailCore 3D printer docs archive.
